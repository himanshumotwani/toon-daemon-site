Privacy policy

Last updated: November 2026.

What this app does

toon-daemon is a private automation that creates a short cartoon video each day and uploads it to its owner's own YouTube channel. It uses Google sign-in only so the owner can authorise those uploads.

Data we collect

None. This app does not collect, store, or share personal data from any user. It has no accounts, no cookies, no analytics, and no tracking.
